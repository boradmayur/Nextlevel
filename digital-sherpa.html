Digital Sherpa Program

Your Path to Higher Impact and Higher Earnings

Next Level is a global authorized distributor of Resulticks and our Digital Sherpas are digital professionals helping businesses adopt and integrate Resulticks’ audience engagement solutions.

Sherpas leverage their expertise to onboard clients, recommend solutions, and help businesses with digital marketing, all while earning financial rewards through commissions and bonuses.

It’s easy to join our Digital Sherpa Program and free to get started!

Why Become a Digital Sherpa?

Certified Sherpas have free access to the latest Resulticks technology and tools while earning attractive compensation for onboarding clients. It’s a win-win for your career and your bottom line!

Our program is built on two core pillars to ensure that our Sherpas are successful:

Financial Rewards

  • Enrollment Bonus: Kickstart your journey with a $500 reward when you complete your certification.
  • Commissions: Earn 30% of the Annual Contract Value (ACV) for new multi-year contracts in the first year, with ongoing commissions of 20% in year two and 10% in year three.
  • Referral Bonuses: Bring others into the Sherpa program and earn $300 for each successful referral.

Growth and Connections

  • Technological Expertise: Enhance your skills in cutting-edge digital solutions like Marketing Star and SmartDX, equipping you to solve real business challenges.
  • Career Development: Improve your business acumen and gain experience that helps you deliver measurable value to clients.
  • Expand Your Network: Join a thriving community of Sherpas, sharing insights and strategies with other professionals.

Who Can Join?

The Digital Sherpa Program is designed for ambitious digital professionals who are passionate about technology and enjoy helping others learn and adopt new tools.

Whether you're just starting out in the digital space or an experienced pro, this program is your gateway to growth and success. We’re excited to collaborate with you—no matter where you are on your journey!

Key Resulticks Solutions

Marketing Star is an intuitive marketing automation solution designed for businesses. Comprehensive, yet simple to use, it is tailored with all the key features businesses require to accelerate multichannel engagement and drive conversions.

SmartDX is a powerful attribution tool that helps you identify visitors and convert them into paying customers through personalized notifications. It enables businesses to track customer journeys and measure the impact of marketing efforts.
